Video Rotate v1.0 — Safari userscript
For Safari + the Userscripts extension. YouTube / Bilibili / any web video.

UI language: follows your browser automatically (Chinese → 中文, otherwise English),
              and you can switch it manually from the panel's last row (中文 / EN).
              Standalone Chinese-only / English-only builds are in dist/ — see section 6.

════════════════════════════════
1. The three icons in the player controls
════════════════════════════════
Where: to the left of YouTube's gear (settings) button; on the right side of Bilibili's controls.

     ⟳    ⚙    ⛶
  rotate panel fullscreen

· ⟳  One click = 90°. Hold Shift to rotate the other way. The icon highlights once rotated.
· ⚙  Opens the settings panel — it takes no space until you need it, and clicking anywhere else closes it.
· ⛶  Fullscreen / exit fullscreen.

[Fullscreen] the three icons move to the BOTTOM-RIGHT corner and stay there (same side as the
player's own control bar), raised just enough to clear YouTube's bar, so the two never overlap when
the control bar slides up on mouse move. The panel opens above these buttons.

════════════════════════════════
2. What this version fixes (the important part)
════════════════════════════════
The old complaint — "in fullscreen I have to fiddle with a dozen settings before it looks right" —
had a single root cause:

  In fullscreen the script treated the small player box on the page (1080×607) as the "display
  area" instead of the whole screen (1080×1920). The picture was therefore computed at about 1/3
  of its proper size: you had to zoom to 300% to fill the screen, and then nudge it up or down to
  get it centred.

How it behaves now:

· In fullscreen (including Safari's "native video fullscreen") the display area is always the whole screen.
· The aspect-ratio presets CROP instead of stretch — 4:3 / 1:1 / 9:16 no longer squash the picture.
· New "Fit screen": picks the right angle, fills the screen, resets zoom to 100% and offset to zero.
· New "Scope" selector: Auto / Player / Screen.
· A live status line at the bottom of the panel (video AR, display area, zoom, crop amount) —
  if something still looks off, that single line is usually enough to pinpoint the cause.

★ For a 1080×1920 portrait screen:
  Windowed   → change nothing (Auto by default, same as before)
  Fullscreen → one click on ⟳ (90°) fills the whole portrait screen; nothing else to adjust.
               Or just open ⚙ → "Fit screen".

3. What's in the panel
════════════════════════════════
  Display
  Angle    [0°] [90°] [180°] [270°]   ← click an angle directly, no repeated rotating
  Aspect   [Auto][Fill][16:9][4:3][1:1][9:16]
  Scope    [Auto][Player][Screen]
  Zoom     −   315%   +                ← click the percentage to snap back to 100%
           ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬●             ← the bar owns a full row: click anywhere = that zoom
  Presets  [50%][75%][100%][125%][150%][200%][315%][400%]
  Position ↑  ↓  ←  →   [Center]
           [ Fit screen ]  [ Reset all ]
  Language [中文] [EN]                 ← remembered per site
           ───────────────────────
           Video 16:9 (Auto box) · Scope 1080×1920 fullscreen · Zoom 100%

Zoom (click the bar and land exactly there — no more tapping + over and over):
  · Bar        The bar owns a whole row (wider, easier to hit). Click anywhere on it and the zoom
               jumps straight to that value; hold and drag to scrub. Want 315%? Click where 315% is.
               (Fastest method — recommended.)
  · Presets    Jump to 50 / 75 / 100 / 125 / 150 / 200 / 315 / 400% in one click; the active value highlights.
  · − / +      5% per click; the further from 100%, the bigger the step (up to 28%).
  · Hold − / + Press and hold: after 240 ms it repeats continuously — roughly 100% → 300% within a
               second — and it stops the moment you release.
  · Wheel      Hover the zoom row or the bar and scroll: ±10% per notch;
               ⇧ + wheel = fine 2%; ⌥ + wheel = coarse 50%.
  · Percentage Click it to snap back to 100%.

Aspect ratios (all CROP, never distort):
  Auto   Fit: the whole picture stays visible, as large as possible (default, recommended)
  Fill   Cover: no letterboxing; whatever overflows the edges is cut off
  16:9 / 4:3 / 1:1 / 9:16
         Crop the picture to that ratio and scale it up as far as it goes (not squashed)
         e.g. a 16:9 video with 9:16 selected → a vertical slice from the middle fills the frame

Scope:
  Auto    Whole screen in fullscreen, player box when windowed (what you normally want)
  Player  Always compute from the player box
  Screen  Always compute from the whole screen — lets you fill the screen even in windowed mode

UI language:
  Follows the browser by default:
    browser is Chinese (zh-CN, zh-Hant, …) → Chinese UI
    anything else                          → English UI
  To change it: last row of the panel, "Language" → click [中文] or [EN].
  A manual choice wins over the browser language and is remembered per site.
  In English the panel widens automatically — English labels (Language / Position) are much longer
  than the Chinese ones, and without that extra width the buttons would be pushed out of the panel.

  Want a standalone Chinese-only or English-only script (language locked, no switch row, slightly
  smaller)? They live in dist/ — see section 6.

4. Keyboard shortcuts
════════════════════════════════
  Option + R               Rotate 90° clockwise
  Option + Shift + R       Rotate 90° counter-clockwise
  Option + 0               Reset everything (0° / Auto / 100%)
  Option + = / -           Zoom in / out (adaptive step; holding the key auto-repeats, quickly)
  Option + Shift + = / -   Jump 50% at a time (to approach a target fast)
  f                        Fullscreen / exit fullscreen (same as YouTube)

════════════════════════════════
5. Nothing happens? (in order)
════════════════════════════════
1. Quit Safari completely (Command + Q) and reopen it — after editing the script this is the only way
   to make the extension re-read it. Reloading the page is not enough.
2. Reload the page with Command + R.
3. A toast saying "Video Rotate v1.0 ready: ⟳ rotate · ⚙ more settings" means the script is running.
   No toast = the extension didn't load the script: click "AA / size" in the address bar →
   Extensions → Userscripts → set to "Always Allow" → reload.

Note: this version changed the settings storage namespace, so anything dialled in by hand previously
no longer applies. That's deliberate — those values were compensating for a bug that no longer exists.
